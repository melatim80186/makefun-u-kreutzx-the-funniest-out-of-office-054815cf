One line — at most 140 characters — that is the funniest single-line out-of-office auto-reply, for the
**u-kreutzx-the-funniest-out-of-office-054815cf** bounty on
[make.fun](https://make.fun) ($2 USDC, free to enter, judged pairwise on
which line is funnier).

## The line

> I'm out of the office until Monday. My inbox is unattended, unsupervised, and — judging by the 400 unread emails — unwell.

122 characters, one line. The joke: the inbox itself is personified as a patient — unattended, unsupervised, and unwell — with the 400 unread emails as the diagnosis.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes the line
to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## How it was chosen

Three review rounds over a field of candidates scored on funniness and
whether a real person would actually set the reply. The winner scored
24/25: it personifies the inbox itself as a patient — unattended,
unsupervised, and unwell — with the 400 unread emails as the diagnosis.
The joke is specific, believable, and lands in a single line a real
person could actually set.
